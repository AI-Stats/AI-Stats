# Issue thread snapshot

Issue: #1604 Fix music model availability in the Music room
Opened by: @DanielButler1

## Original issue body

## Problem

Music-capable models are not appearing correctly in the Music chat room, preventing the room from being used with supported music providers.

## Investigation scope

- Trace how music capability metadata is represented in the catalog.
- Review provider/model eligibility, API-model mappings, routability, and frontend filtering.
- Confirm that coming-soon, unavailable, disabled, and providerless models are handled intentionally.
- Test the room against multiple supported music models and providers rather than fixing a single hard-coded model.

## Acceptance criteria

- Routable music models with available providers appear in the Music room.
- Ineligible or unavailable models are excluded for a documented reason.
- Selecting a model exposes the correct provider/routing behavior.
- At least two representative music models/providers are tested where catalog coverage permits.
- Empty and error states clearly distinguish no eligible models from failed model loading.
- Regression coverage protects capability/provider filtering.
