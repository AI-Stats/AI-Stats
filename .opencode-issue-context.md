# Issue thread snapshot

Issue: #1603 Audit and align all chat room experiences
Opened by: @DanielButler1

## Original issue body

## Goal

Perform a complete design and interaction audit of every chat room and bring the non-text experiences into the same coherent product family as the primary text chat room.

The rooms should not become visually identical where their workflows genuinely differ, but shared states, controls, feedback patterns, and design language should feel intentional and consistent.

## Rooms in scope

At minimum, review:

- Text chat as the reference experience.
- Audio.
- Realtime.
- Image generation.
- Video generation.
- General media generation.
- Embeddings.
- Moderation.
- Any other chat-room variants, shared room shells, or creation experiences currently exposed in the product.

## Audit areas

### Creation and processing states

- Use the established orb treatment, motion language, and progress feedback during generation or creation where appropriate.
- Review idle, submitting, queued, streaming, processing, retrying, completed, cancelled, and failed states.
- Ensure transitions do not cause avoidable layout shifts or leave stale controls active.
- Keep progress language and loading indicators appropriate to each modality.

### Dialogs, sheets, menus, and overlays

- Review every dialog, sheet, popover, dropdown, confirmation, and settings surface opened from a room.
- Align hierarchy, spacing, radii, typography, buttons, destructive actions, close behavior, focus management, and responsive presentation.
- Ensure complex modality-specific settings remain understandable rather than merely matching text chat visually.

### Color and visual-system consistency

- Audit colors, contrast, borders, backgrounds, muted states, selected states, hover/focus states, status colors, and gradients.
- Verify light and dark themes.
- Remove unexplained one-off styling where existing design tokens or shared components should be used.
- Confirm provider/model branding does not undermine legibility or accessibility.

### Layout and responsive behavior

- Review desktop, tablet, and mobile layouts.
- Check room headers, sidebars, composers, generated output, controls, empty states, and scrolling behavior.
- Ensure safe areas, keyboard interaction, long content, and narrow viewports are handled cleanly.
- Preserve modality-specific space requirements for image, video, audio, and realtime experiences.

### Shared behavior and accessibility

- Align model/provider selectors, settings access, action placement, retry/cancel/delete behavior, errors, and empty states where the underlying action is equivalent.
- Verify keyboard navigation, focus visibility, accessible names, status announcements, reduced-motion behavior, and touch targets.
- Identify duplicated room UI that should use existing shared primitives without forcing unlike workflows into a single abstraction.

## Deliverables

- A documented room-by-room audit with screenshots or concise findings.
- A consistency matrix showing shared patterns and justified modality-specific differences.
- Fixes for small, tightly related inconsistencies that can safely ship together.
- Separate follow-up issues for larger redesigns or behavioral changes discovered during the audit.
- Regression coverage for shared behavior changed as part of the work.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every active chat room has been reviewed across its principal lifecycle states.
- Creation periods use a coherent orb/loading treatment where appropriate.
- Dialogs and related overlays follow the current design system and work responsively.
- Light and dark theme colors meet expected contrast and state clarity.
- Equivalent actions and states use consistent patterns across rooms.
- Mobile and keyboard workflows have been verified.
- Remaining intentional differences and follow-up work are documented.
